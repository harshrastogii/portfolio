AI Will Not Build Your Startup For You

One of the biggest misconceptions today is that having access to AI means you can build anything. After spending months building with AI every day, I have learned that reality is far more complicated.

Over the past few months, I have spent countless hours building products with AI.

Like many founders, I use AI for almost everything. Coding, brainstorming, writing, research, debugging, planning, problem-solving, and even challenging my own assumptions.

There is no doubt that AI has made me significantly more productive.

But recently, after spending an entire day working alongside AI, I realised something that I don't see enough people talking about.

AI will not build your startup for you.

The Biggest Misconception

Every day I see people online claiming that AI has changed everything.

People say that if you have Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other AI tool, you can build anything.

That sounds great in theory.

The reality is very different.

AI can write code.

AI can explain concepts.

AI can help you brainstorm.

AI can help you move faster.

But AI does not replace creativity, judgment, taste, or vision.

In fact, sometimes it actively works against them.

AI Loves Safe Decisions

One thing I have noticed repeatedly is that AI naturally pushes you towards safer decisions.

Whenever I wanted to try something experimental, different, or unconventional, the response was often the same.

Be careful.

That might create risk.

That could cause issues.

Maybe we should avoid that.

Maybe we should keep things simple.

Maybe we should focus on stability.

None of these suggestions were wrong.

The problem is that startups are not built entirely through safe decisions.

If every founder only followed the safest possible path, innovation would barely exist.

Experimentation is part of the process.

Trying things that might fail is part of the process.

Building something unique is part of the process.

AI understands optimisation.

It does not always understand experimentation.

Sometimes AI Creates The Barrier

The surprising thing is that sometimes AI becomes the thing slowing you down.

You ask it to help implement an idea.

Instead of helping you test the idea, it starts explaining why the idea might not work.

You ask it to build something.

It starts creating additional steps, additional checks, additional discussions, and additional caution.

Again, those things have value.

But after a certain point, they become barriers rather than safeguards.

As founders, we already know there are risks.

We already know things can break.

Sometimes we simply want to test an idea and see what happens.

That willingness to experiment is often where the best ideas come from.

AI Is Not The Source Of Creativity

This is probably the biggest lesson I have learned.

AI is incredibly powerful.

But AI is not the source of creativity.

The founder is.

The founder decides what problem is worth solving.

The founder decides what is worth building.

The founder decides what is worth experimenting with.

AI can help execute those ideas.

AI can improve them.

AI can challenge them.

AI can even point out flaws.

But it cannot replace the creative spark that started the idea in the first place.

The Real Skill

I no longer think the biggest advantage is having access to AI.

Millions of people have access to the same models.

The real advantage is knowing how to work with them.

Knowing when to listen.

Knowing when to challenge.

Knowing when to push back.

Knowing when the AI is right.

And knowing when the AI is simply being too cautious.

The people who will get the most value from AI are not the people who blindly follow every recommendation.

They are the people who treat AI like a highly capable assistant rather than an all-knowing decision maker.

Final Thoughts

AI has completely changed the way I build.

I would not want to build without it.

It has helped me learn faster, move faster, and accomplish things that would have taken significantly longer in the past.

But one thing has become very clear.

Having AI does not mean you can build anything.

Having AI does not automatically make you a founder.

Having AI does not replace creativity.

The technology is powerful.

The judgment still has to come from you.

And sometimes, the best thing you can do is tell the AI to stop talking and help you build.
