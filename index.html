NT Protected-Area Coverage

Live

A full-stack spatial analysis of whether the Territory's parks protect a representative cross-section of its land. The reserve network over-represents rugged northern country and under-protects the arid interior — the Tanami, the NT's largest bioregion at ~538,000 km², is a statistically significant cold spot at just 0.6% coverage.
